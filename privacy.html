Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 14, 2026  |  Last updated: October 8, 2026

Stokita ("we", "our", "us", or "the app") is an inventory and orders management application for iOS and Android, operated by Emre Macit. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, how we share it, and the choices you have regarding your data. By using Stokita, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

On iOS, you sign in with Apple. On Android, you sign in with Google. Depending on the sign-in provider, we receive:

  • A unique Apple or Google user identifier and a Stokita account identifier
  • Your name, where provided by the sign-in provider or entered in your profile
  • Your email address (Apple may provide a private relay email if you choose)

1.2 Business & Application Data

When you use Stokita, you may create and store the following business data:

  • Organization/workspace name and settings
  • Products, variants, stock levels, barcodes, and product images
  • Customers, suppliers, and contact information you enter
  • Orders, purchase invoices, expenses, and inventory logs
  • Team member names and assigned permissions
  • To-do lists and internal notes

This data is owned by you. We process it only to provide the app's functionality.

1.3 Subscription & Purchase Data

Subscription purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store on iOS, or Google through Google Play on Android. We do not receive or store your card details. We receive purchase records needed to validate and manage subscriptions, including:

  • Subscription status (active, expired, cancelled)
  • Subscription plan and renewal date
  • Store transaction identifiers or Google Play purchase tokens (for validation)

RevenueCat processes purchase history, subscription status and your Stokita account identifier to validate subscriptions, manage access and provide subscription analytics. We do not send your product images or customer contact records to RevenueCat.

1.4 Technical Information

For security, performance, and debugging, we may automatically collect:

  • Device model, operating system version, app version and SDK performance or diagnostic information
  • IP address (temporarily, for request authentication)
  • Technical error information. Google's Android barcode scanning SDK may collect device and app information, installation or device identifiers, scan-session identifiers, performance metrics and diagnostics for service operation and usage analytics. Automatic zoom also produces zoom events and the predicted barcode bounding-box coordinates

The Android barcode scanner processes camera images on the device; Stokita uses the returned barcode value. Product images you deliberately select for upload are sent to our Cloudflare storage. We do not access your device address book, email inbox or location permissions.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the app's core functionality
  • Authenticate your account and protect it from unauthorized access
  • Validate and manage your subscription
  • Sync your data across your devices
  • Communicate with you about service updates, security alerts, or support requests
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues or abuse
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your data for advertising. We do not train AI/ML models on your content.

3. How We Store & Protect Your Data

Your data is stored on Cloudflare infrastructure (Workers, D1 database, and R2 object storage). Cloudflare maintains industry-standard security measures including encryption at rest and in transit (TLS).

  • Access tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain or encrypted with an Android Keystore key on your device
  • We do not receive your Apple or Google account password; sign-in is handled by the respective provider
  • All communication between the app and our servers uses HTTPS
  • Access to production data is limited to authorized personnel

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We share data only with the following service providers, strictly to operate the app:

  • Apple — Sign in with Apple authentication, App Store subscription processing
  • Google — Google sign-in authentication, Google Play subscription processing, and Android barcode scanning services
  • Cloudflare — Hosting, database, and image storage
  • RevenueCat — Subscription validation and management

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Multi-Tenant Data

Stokita supports multiple users within an organization. If you join an organization:

  • Organization administrators can see your name, email, role, and actions within the organization
  • Data you create inside an organization is visible to other authorized members based on their permissions
  • Administrators can deactivate your access and manage organization data

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account in the app, your Stokita account profile, memberships and refresh tokens are removed. Shared organization records are not automatically deleted when an individual member deletes their account. Deletion requests for associated data held by our service providers can be sent to support. We handle deletion requests within 30 days, except where information must be retained for legal, tax or fraud prevention purposes. Deleting your Stokita account does not automatically cancel a store subscription; cancel it through Apple or Google Play.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Export your data
  • Withdraw consent or object to processing
  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority

To exercise these rights, contact us at e.macit1130@gmail.com. You can delete your Stokita account from Settings → Delete Account. If you are an organization's sole administrator, you must first appoint another administrator or contact support to resolve the organization. You may also request account and data deletion without using the app at our account deletion page.

8. Children's Privacy

Stokita is a business tool and is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own. By using the app, you consent to the transfer of your data to these locations. We take reasonable steps to ensure your data is protected wherever it is processed.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date and, where appropriate, notify you through the app. Continued use of the app after changes become effective constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
